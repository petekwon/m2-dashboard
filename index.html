Interpretation

    Data policy

    In-browser: BTC & ETH prices (CoinGecko), 200W MA from weekly candles (Binance.US, Coinbase fallback).

    Scheduled feed: 10Y yield (FRED), Global M2 proxy (FRED + ECB), BTC/ETH ETF flows (SoSoValue).

    Each metric carries its own observation date. Stale after 4 days (ETF), 5 days (10Y), 8 days (200W MA) or 100 days (M2, which publishes with a lag).

    Input Health

    Green = usable, yellow = stale/suspicious, red = broken/missing. Live feeds also show the most recent successful fetch.
    Sources: CoinGecko (prices), Binance.US / Coinbase (weekly candles), SoSoValue (ETF flows), FRED + ECB (M2), FRED DGS10 (10Y yield).
    This dashboard is a market-monitoring aid, not investment advice.
